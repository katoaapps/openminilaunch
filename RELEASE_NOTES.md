Open 4.0.1 brings the latest MinkBoard card workflows and physical-keyboard controls to the embedded To-Dos experience.

## To-Dos and MinkBoard

- **Faster card completion:** Mark done now appears first in the card overflow menu.
- **More natural dragging:** Long-press anywhere on a card to begin dragging, while the dedicated drag handle remains available.
- **Safer subtask editing:** Pressing Done saves an active subtask edit or valid pending subtask before closing the dialog.
- **Physical keyboard navigation:** Use Shift+N to create a card, Shift+W/A/S/D or directional equivalents to move the card highlight, and Shift+Enter to open the highlighted card.
- **Consistent card emphasis:** Keyboard selection, active focus timers, and jump/reveal feedback retain high-contrast white outlines across themes.

This maintenance release does not change OpenMink's permissions, backup schema, analytics policy, or network behavior.

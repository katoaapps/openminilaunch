Open 3.2.1 is a focused Home, Conversations, and ambient-display update built on the 3.2.0 feature release.

- **Adrift ambient mode:** Long-press the keyboard button on the Home Magic Box to enter a full-screen, orientation-aware collage made from Memories photos and animated GIFs. Independently fading frames fill the display around a centered clock, while videos remain excluded. A Favorite-conversation indicator appears only when attention is pending, and Back returns to Adrift after opening Conversations.
- **A real Mink Theme:** The default theme now uses Mink's brown-and-green palette across Home, Settings, and the launcher keyboard. Bundled themes still pair wallpaper, background, and pill colors; Device wallpaper preserves the user's chosen photo so it can be restored after trying a theme.
- **Readable colors on the actual background:** Home labels, secondary captions, controls, status/navigation bars, and launcher-keyboard colors adapt to the visible wallpaper or theme instead of assuming the selected source is still on screen. Semantic action colors and Favorite notification counts keep their intended high-contrast colors.
- **Home uses the space around camera cutouts:** The date, time, battery, Conversations, and Settings controls can occupy usable space beside centered or side cutouts instead of pushing the entire launcher downward. Individual header items move and align as a group only when the reported cutout requires it.
- **Fewer ghost Favorite alerts:** Conversations now reconciles acknowledged and removed notifications against Android's active notification set so old or superseded messages do not randomly return as unread attention.
- **Portable Favorite attention colors:** Android Favorite colors can be matched best-effort after restore with export-scoped opaque tokens. Names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact IDs, photos, message contents, and relationship data are not copied into that metadata; ambiguous or missing matches are left unresolved.

The permanent **Join Google Play beta** button remains in Quick Drawer and is not replaced by What's New. No new permissions, analytics, accounts, or network services are introduced. Portable backup schema version 3 remains unchanged, older schema-3 backups continue to import, and selected Memories media remains device-bound.

MinkLauncher Open 1.5.3 adds device-sized image wallpapers with an in-app pinch-and-drag crop screen. The finished crop stays on-device and is applied to both Mink Home and Android’s Home wallpaper while leaving the lock screen unchanged. This release also adds pill transparency, independent date and clock controls, cleaner See All labels, and refreshed appearance onboarding. Wallpaper images are not included in portable JSON backups.

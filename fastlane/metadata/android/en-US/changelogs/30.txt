MinkLauncher OpenSource 3.0 introduces MinkPad, a native Bluetooth keyboard, trackpad, media controller, and TV remote for Android 9 or newer. Pair and switch between saved receiving devices, use hardware or software text input, and tune pointer and scrolling behavior.

MinkSpace can now host an Android widget as a mini-app with setup, replacement, sizing guidance, and an interaction mode for nested controls. Every mini-app is optional and reorderable, while To-dos remain reachable from Settings and the Magic Box.

Settings now uses adaptive sections for compact and large displays. Appearance adds sixteen bundled wallpapers, reusable personal photos, custom local fonts, live text sizing, and font colors.

This release also improves physical-keyboard hotkeys, directional trackpad navigation, large-font widget setup, wallpaper application, widget refresh behavior, and controller usability.

MinkLauncher Open 1.5 adds Nova-compatible icon packs with previews, dynamic calendar artwork, customizable Mink icon colors, and grouped appearance controls. App discovery now supports launcher shortcuts, clearer app labels, direct launch from results, and App info from See All. It also adds optional clock display, Beeper and Molly messaging choices, smoother icon-application feedback, and improved physical-keyboard input without an idle IME toolbar. No new permission is required.

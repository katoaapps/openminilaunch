MinkLauncher Open 1.5.1 upgrades Magic Box messaging with recent-chat shortcuts from installed providers, provider icons, and exact-chat draft handoffs where supported. Signal and Molly can receive contact-addressed drafts. Unsaved phone numbers and usernames can be tried as BETA recipients for messages and calls. Manual messaging keeps all provider shortcuts visible while placing the chosen app first. This release also improves routing fallbacks and internal code organization. No new permission is required.

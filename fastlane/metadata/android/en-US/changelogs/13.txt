MinkLauncher OpenSource 1.0 brings the current responsive Home layout, a three-task preview, Mink’s Day insights for selected social apps, and Android-standard email notifications in Conversations. Selected social apps stay visible for quick removal, while Android’s Social defaults remain easy to restore.

Document search now uses only folders you explicitly select through Android. MinkLauncher OpenSource no longer requests All files access. No new permission is required.

MinkLauncher Open 1.5.2 adds portable local backup and restore for moving settings, launcher layout, app choices, icon preferences, Mink’s Day configuration, and to-dos between F-Droid and GitHub installs. Android permissions, roles, widgets, accessibility and notification access, and document-folder grants remain device-managed and must be configured again. This release also adds a reviewed AI provider catalog, provider-owned installation links, and a clearly labeled copy-and-paste handoff for Lumo. No new permission is required.

MinkLauncher OpenSource 1.3.5 adds optional launcher barriers to Mink’s Day. Choose a whole-hour daily limit from 0 to 23 and pause selected apps all the time, only after their collective limit, or never. Paused apps disappear from Magic Box and app drawers; shortcut tiles remain visible with a reminder.

Home can now hide the status bar, compact square-screen layouts fit more tasks, and browser selection recognizes more installed browsers. No new permission is required.
